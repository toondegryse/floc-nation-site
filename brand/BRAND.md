# Floc Nation brand sheet

One page for anyone making Floc Nation material: posts, video thumbnails, slides, stickers or the website. It follows the Brand section of `CLAUDE.md`. If the two ever differ, `CLAUDE.md` wins.

## Name

**Floc Nation**, always spelled without a K. It comes from flocculation: yeast clumping together and dropping out, so the beer clears.

## Logo

The "three hops" badge: three teal hop cones above a navy banner, "FLOC" in white, "NATION" in sky.

- **File:** `brand/logo/floc-nation-badge.svg`. The website uses an identical copy, `docs/assets/floc-nation-badge.svg`. For profile pictures, use `brand/social/profile-picture-1080.png`.
- **Use it as it is.** The lettering is artwork, not a font. Never retype it.
- **Clear space:** at least a quarter of the badge's height on every side. Keep text, edges and other images out of that space.
- **Minimum size:** 120 px wide on screen, 25 mm wide in print. Smaller than that, "NATION" gets hard to read. Platforms show profile pictures smaller; that is out of our hands.
- **Background:** plain white `#FDFDFD` or pale blue `#EEF4F8`. On a navy or dark background, the badge's navy banner disappears into it. Put the badge on a white panel instead, as the YouTube banner and Facebook cover do.

**Don't:**

- redraw it, or rebuild it from other parts
- change its colours
- stretch or squash it
- crop or rotate it
- add effects: shadows, glows, outlines, gradients
- add words or shapes to it
- place it on busy photos or patterns

## Colours

All taken from the badge.

| Colour | Hex | Use |
|---|---|---|
| Navy | `#022C54` | Text, header and footer backgrounds, dark panels |
| Hop teal | `#06929E` | Accents, rules, icons, and text 24 px or larger on white |
| Deep teal | `#06727B` | Links and small accent text on white |
| Sky | `#2BACC7` | Accents and links on navy only |
| White | `#FDFDFD` | Page background, text on navy |
| Pale blue | `#EEF4F8` | Cards and panels on the white page |
| Line | `#D5DEE6` | Borders and dividers only, never text |
| Muted text | `#4F5B6B` | Secondary text on white or pale blue |

**Text on white (or pale blue):** navy, deep teal and muted text at any size. Hop teal only at 24 px or larger. Never sky or line: they are too light.

**Text on navy:** white at any size, and sky for links and accents. Not hop teal, deep teal or muted text: they are too dark.

Contrast ratios behind these rules, measured on white `#FDFDFD`: navy 13.9:1, muted text 6.8:1, deep teal 5.6:1, hop teal 3.7:1, sky 2.6:1. On navy: white 13.9:1, sky 5.3:1.

## Type

**Manrope**, free under the SIL Open Font Licence.

- ExtraBold (800) for headlines.
- Medium (500) and SemiBold (600) for everything else.

## Words

- **Tagline:** "Brewed and built in Bangkok"
- **Bio line on every channel:** "Brewed and built in Bangkok. Home brewing problems and the tools that fix them."
- **Voice:** practical and plain. Talk about real brewing problems and how they get fixed. Short sentences. No hype words.
- **Look:** professional and calm, like the badge. Not playful, not kid-like.
- **Topics:** the hobby, the automations and the community. No beer sales, prices or legal topics.
- **Privacy:** no personal details on the website: no personal names, addresses, phone numbers or personal email addresses.
