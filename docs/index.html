About Floc Nation

Floc Nation brews beer at home in Bangkok and builds its own tools along the way. We share what goes wrong, why, and how it gets fixed. The name comes from flocculation: yeast clumping together and dropping out, so the beer clears.

What we work on

  • Automation.

    Tools that take the slow, error-prone steps out of brewing. One is a dextrose dispenser, because measuring priming sugar by hand is fiddly. We're still building it.

  • Merch.

    A Floc Nation T-shirt for people who brew. It isn't available yet.

  • Brew days.

    A batch brewed from start to finish. This is where problems show up and fixes get tested. We share brew days online. They aren't open to visitors.
