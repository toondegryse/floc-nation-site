Priming-sugar calculator

How much sugar to add at bottling, for the whole batch and for each bottle.

Too little sugar gives flat beer. Too much gives gushers, or bottles that burst. The right amount depends on how much beer you have, how fizzy you want it and how warm it got after fermentation.
